\section{Midpoint Assessment}
Our project now studies M1 DMP exploitation through both Augury and GoFetch. The completed experimental effort has focused on Augury activation and reverse engineering; the timing logs summarized here provide an initial measurement record rather than a complete quantitative characterization. The remaining work connects that characterization to exploit demonstrations and to controlled Asahi Linux and M4 comparisons. This progression will let us distinguish what we reproduced, what changed across platforms, and what remains unexplained without overstating the preliminary evidence.
